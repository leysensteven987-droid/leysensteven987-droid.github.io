EU Orders Google to Give Claude and ChatGPT the Same Android System Access as Gemini - by July 2027

A July 16 European Commission DMA ruling requires Google to grant qualifying third-party AI assistants - including Claude, ChatGPT, and others - equal system-level Android privileges: custom wake word activation, long-press home button invocation, full screen-context reading, and in-app action execution. These capabilities have been exclusively Gemini's. Implementation is required in the next major Android version, expected July 2027, and compliance obligations run even while Google appeals, since DMA appeals do not automatically suspend enforcement.

What's moving
  • The Commission simultaneously ordered Google to share anonymized query, click, and ranking data with rival search engines and AI chatbot providers starting January 2027 - pairing Android system access with a data-sharing mandate that could reshape how AI products train on real user signals
  • Android runs on roughly 72% of mobile devices globally, making this the highest-stakes regulatory intervention yet in the competition for who controls AI at the operating-system layer
  • Google must build mandatory interoperability features on a court-supervised timeline while simultaneously appealing, putting engineering teams under dual pressure - and creating a template Brussels could use to force the same access on Apple's iOS

Beijing Moves to License Frontier AI Exports - Treating Model Weights and Training Pipelines as National Assets

China's Ministry of Commerce published enforcement guidance on July 9 requiring export licenses before model weights, training pipelines, or inference infrastructure can be transferred to foreign entities, following closed-door meetings on July 7 with Alibaba, ByteDance, and Z.ai.
