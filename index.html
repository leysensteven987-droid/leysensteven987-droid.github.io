How the hell do I pronounce Kage-Gumi?
kah·geh  goo·mee
kahas in "car", drop the r
gehas in "get", drop the t
gooas in "goo"
meeas in "me"

Hard g both times, and the same weight on every syllable. Not "cage gummy".
影 kage, shadow · 組 gumi, crew.

But KG (kay-gee) is equally acceptable.
